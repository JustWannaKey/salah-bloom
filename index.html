Salah Bloom is a free app offered as it is. There is no support service and replies are not guaranteed, but messages about privacy or serious problems are read.

Most questions are answered below.

How do I stop an alarm?

Scan your own Salah Bloom code. That is the only way, which is the point of the app. If it is not scanned, the alarm stops by itself when that prayer’s time ends.

I lost my printed code.

Open Salah Bloom when no alarm is ringing, go to Settings → Make a new code, and print it. The old code stops working.

An alarm didn’t ring.

Check that notifications for Salah Bloom are allowed and that a Focus mode isn’t silencing it (allow Time Sensitive notifications for Salah Bloom). Open Salah Bloom at least every day or so; iPhones limit how many alarms an app can schedule ahead, and opening the app tops them up.

The times differ from my mosque.

Choose a different calculation method in Settings → Prayer times, or turn on Hanafi Asr. Where they still differ, please follow your mosque.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Salah Bloom downloads two months of prayer times at once and can calculate them on the phone when offline. Daylight-saving changes are already included.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all my data, or delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Privacy

Salah Bloom has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no servers of its own. Your settings and prayer history stay on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is an approximate location (about 1 km), sent to a prayer-times service to look up times. Read the privacy policy.
