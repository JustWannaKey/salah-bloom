Salah Bloom Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Salah Bloom has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no servers of its own. Your settings and prayer history stay on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is an approximate location (about 1 km), sent to a prayer-times service to look up times.

Who we are

Salah Bloom is a prayer alarm app made by Servant of Allah. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. Contact: puny-winder.5o@icloud.com.

The short version

Salah Bloom has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no servers of its own. Your settings and prayer history stay on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is an approximate location (about 1 km), sent to a prayer-times service to look up times.

Information kept on your phone

Your prayer settings, alarm sounds, prayer history, points and garden, your personal QR code, and an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) are stored only on your phone, inside the app.

We never receive this information. It is removed when you use “Delete all my data” in Settings or delete the app. Like other app data, it can be included in your phone’s own backups (for example an encrypted iCloud backup), which Apple manages under its own policy.

Location

If you allow location, Salah Bloom reads your approximate position only while you are using the app and rounds it to about 1 km.

To get prayer times, those rounded coordinates are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to the free AlAdhan prayer-times service (aladhan.com), about once a month or when you move to a new area. No name, account, device ID or other identifier is sent. AlAdhan’s terms and privacy notice (aladhan.com/credits-and-terms) apply to that request.

If you type a city instead, your phone’s built-in Apple geocoding service turns the name into coordinates, under Apple’s privacy policy. Salah Bloom never uses location in the background and never tracks where you go.

If you are offline, prayer times are calculated on your phone and nothing is sent.

Camera

The camera is used only to read your Salah Bloom QR code. Images are processed on your phone in real time. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.

Notifications

Alarms are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Salah Bloom does not use push notifications or any notification server.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell, share or rent personal information. We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and we do not combine your information with data from other companies.

Children

Salah Bloom is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can decline location and type a city instead, turn off camera or notification access in your phone’s Settings at any time, and erase everything with Settings → Delete all my data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same web address and in the app, with a new date.

Effective date: 25 November 2026.

Please note

Prayer times are calculated estimates based on the method you choose. Your local mosque’s timetable may differ by a few minutes. Please follow it where it differs.

Salah Bloom is a reminder tool. Phones can silence, delay or block alarms (for example Focus modes, low battery, or the app being removed from memory), so it cannot guarantee that every alarm will ring.

Qur’an and hadith passages are paraphrased meanings for encouragement, with their sources. They are not formal translations or religious rulings.
